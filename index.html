Saving deposits lost to technical glitches

Problem
A brief backend failure failed the customer's fixed deposit and turned it into a support ticket with a 1–2 day wait.
What I did
Dug into the failure funnel with ops and backend teams and found ~70% of failures fixed themselves within minutes. Redesigned the journey to retry quietly in the background and took it from spec to launch.
Key decision
Retry first, and raise a ticket only after every retry fails, so most customers never see the failure.
~80%expected drop in a targeted fraud pattern

Protecting elderly customers from deposit fraud

Problem
Fraudsters talked elderly customers into closing their fixed deposits and moving the money out right away. Each step looked normal on its own, so standard checks missed it.
What I did
Traced the pattern through support and ops data and defined the risk signals with our fraud analytics partner. Then designed Trusted Guardian: an elderly customer names someone they trust, who can verify flagged payments before the money moves.
Key decision
Bring in the guardian only for flagged cases, not every payment, so everyday banking stays smooth.

Business rule changes without a release

Problem
Deposit closure and overdraft creation ran on two near-identical APIs. Changing even an interest-rate rule took code, a sprint slot and a release: 1–2 weeks.
What I did
Designed one API for both journeys with rules moved from code into config, and shipped an admin portal where the business team edits them directly.
Key decision
Merge the two APIs instead of patching each one: more work upfront, but no engineering dependency for future regulatory changes.

upsorb

Jun 2026 – now · live

An AI homework platform for any classroom, from schools to coaching centres. Teachers set homework in minutes. Students get an AI tutor that guides them with hints instead of giving answers. Teachers see how each student learns.

What I did

  • Ran 50+ discovery interviews with teachers, students and coaching centres to set strategy and go-to-market
  • Built and shipped the whole product myself with Claude Code: a web app, an Android app and a RAG-backed tutor
  • Logged token cost on every AI call to measure real cost per student, then cut tutor cost per message from ₹0.36 to about ₹0.03 by switching models

Decisions and why

  • Hints, not answers. Students already use AI to skip the thinking. A step-by-step hint tutor keeps them doing the work and keeps teachers in charge.
  • Quality over cost. Lower model reasoning missed student mistakes, so I kept it at medium and tuned it until the first reply came in 1–4 seconds instead of about 19.
  • Pricing per student with a minimum seat count, because fixed server costs made small classes run at a loss.
